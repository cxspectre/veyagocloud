Privacy Policy

Veyago travel app & website · Last updated 4 June 2026

In short. Veyago is a travel destination discovery app and website. We collect only what we need, never sell your personal data, and you can access or delete your information at any time. Looking for the Kept privacy policy? Kept stores everything on your device and collects nothing.

Veyago is a travel destination discovery app and website. This policy explains how Veyago Inc. (“Veyago”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use our mobile apps (iOS and Android) and our website at veyago.app (together, the “Service”).

Data controller

Veyago Inc., a New York corporation. Contact: hello@veyago.cloud.

Data protection contact

Until we appoint a formal Data Protection Officer, all privacy questions are handled directly by our CEO and founder, Cassian Drefke, at hello@veyago.cloud.

UK representative (Article 27 UK GDPR)

If you are in the United Kingdom and wish to contact our UK representative, email hello@veyago.cloud with “UK Representative” in the subject line and we will route your request accordingly.

The company

Veyago Inc. is a New York business corporation, incorporated April 28, 2026 (DOS ID 7899587). Registered agent for service of process in Albany, NY. Founder and CEO: Cassian Drefke.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

  • Account & contact details - such as your name and email address.
  • Travel preferences & activity - destinations, searches, saved places, and lists, to personalize discovery.
  • Approximate or precise location - only with your permission; you can turn it off anytime.
  • Device & usage data - app version, device type, language, and anonymised analytics.
  • Communications - messages you send us and our replies.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Service.
  • To personalize recommendations and remember your preferences.
  • To respond to your messages and provide support.
  • To keep the Service secure and debug issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Contract - to deliver features you ask for.
  • Consent - for optional things like precise location; withdraw anytime.
  • Legitimate interests - to improve and secure the Service.
  • Legal obligation - where the law requires it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers under contract - cloud hosting, mapping and place data, and analytics - and only as needed. We may disclose information if required by law. None of our providers may buy your identity.

International transfers

We operate from the United States, so your data is processed in the United States, with appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed or as required by law. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize the associated data within a reasonable period.

Your rights

Subject to your location, you may access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent - email hello@veyago.cloud. EEA/UK residents may complain to a supervisory authority. California residents may exercise CCPA/CPRA rights; we do not sell or “share” personal information under California law.

Cookies & analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and privacy-respecting analytics. You can control cookies in your browser.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@veyago.cloud.
