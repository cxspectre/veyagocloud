What Unites Them

Different jobs. The same principles.

Whether you are keeping a warranty or finding a destination, the foundation is identical — quiet, private and built to feel effortless.

No accounts, no tracking, no servers holding your life. Your information stays on your device and syncs only through your own private iCloud.

The smart parts run where your data already lives. Apple Intelligence reads, sorts and suggests right on your iPhone — never in the cloud.
