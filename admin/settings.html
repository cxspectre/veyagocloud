    Email

    A record of what was sent. Invites, password resets and task notifications go out through Resend so they arrive branded rather than as plain Supabase mail — if a send fails it shows here, because an invite that never arrives is otherwise invisible. Delivery itself is configured outside the admin, with RESEND_API_KEY set as a Supabase secret from a terminal.
