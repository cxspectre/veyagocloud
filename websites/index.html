Websites

Your business, properly online.

We build websites for businesses anywhere in the world - landing pages, marketing sites, full corporate sites, and the dashboards and CRMs behind them. Fast, findable, and held to the same bar as our own products.

SEO HTML & CSS React Dashboards & CRM

What we build

From one page to the whole back office.

Landing pages

One page, one job - explain what you do and get the enquiry. Quick to ship, quick to change when the offer changes.

Marketing & corporate sites

Multi-page sites with services, case studies, careers and contact - structured so visitors and search engines both find what they came for.

SEO

Technical SEO, clean markup, structured data, sensible page speed and content that answers real searches. Findable, not stuffed with keywords.

Hand-written HTML, CSS & JS

No builder bloat, no plugin tower that breaks on update. Static sites that load fast, cost almost nothing to host, and still work in five years.

React & modern front-ends

When a site needs real interaction - configurators, portals, live data - we reach for React. When it doesn't, we don't. The tool follows the job.

Dashboards, CRMs & admin

The part customers never see: logins, client dashboards, a CRM that fits how you actually sell, invoicing, internal tools. Built on the same stack we run our own business on.

Already have a site?

If AI or a builder got you halfway, we'll take it the rest.

Plenty of businesses already have a website. It came out of Wix, Squarespace, Lovable or a single AI prompt, it looked fine for a week, and now it's slow, impossible to edit, invisible on Google, or simply not the business you actually run. That's the job we take most often.

We rebuild it properly: keep what's working, fix the structure underneath, carry your content and your rankings across, and hand you something you own outright rather than rent. If your existing site is genuinely fine, we'll tell you that too.

Pricing

Websites start at $699, not $2,000.

The going rate for a business website sits at around $2,000, or €2,250 - out of reach for a lot of the businesses that need one most. Ours start at $699, or €750, excluding tax. You get the full number before we start, not after, and it doesn't move unless the scope does.

Why we're cheaper

Because we'd rather have a business that comes back than one invoice that hurt. Fair pricing is how you build a relationship that lasts longer than a single project.

No agency overhead

Small studio, no account managers, no layers between you and the people writing the code. You're not paying for anyone's sales floor.

No surprises

One quote, in writing, with what's included and what isn't. No padded hours, no charge for a change of mind on a colour.

How it goes

Three steps, no ceremony.

1

Talk

Tell us what your business does, who you want to reach, and what the current site gets wrong. We come back with a scope and a fixed price - free, and without a sales call in disguise.

2

Build

Design and build together, with something real to look at early. Static HTML or React depending on what the site needs, SEO handled as we go rather than bolted on at the end.

3

Launch

We ship it, hand over the keys, and show you how to edit it. Want us to keep looking after it? That's an option, never a condition.
