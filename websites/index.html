Questions

What people ask before they write.

How long does it take?

A Launch page is usually live 7 to 10 working days after we have your content. A Business site takes two to four weeks, a Back office project four to eight. The clock starts when the text, photos and logo are in, so the fastest thing you can do is gather those first.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, whatever text you have (rough notes are fine), photos if you have good ones, and access to your domain. If you would rather we write the pages from your notes, that is the copywriting extra. We handle everything else, including the hosting account and the search set-up.

Do I own the website?

Yes, all of it. The code lives in a repository in your name, the hosting runs in your own account and the domain stays yours. There is no monthly fee unless you choose the care plan, and you can hand the site to anyone else at any time. We build it so that you never need us again, then hope you come back anyway.

What does hosting cost?

For most small business sites, nothing. A hand-written static site fits comfortably in the free tier at Vercel or Cloudflare, and we set it up in your own account rather than ours. The domain is the only recurring cost, typically $10 to $20 a year from your registrar.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. Text and images are plain files, and at launch we walk you through changing them. If you expect frequent updates, a news section you can edit is included in Business, and the care plan covers small changes for you.

I already have a site. Will I lose my Google rankings?

Not if the move is done properly, which is most of what a rebuild is. We carry your content across, keep the addresses that already rank, redirect any that change, and register the new site in Search Console before it goes live. If your current site is genuinely fine we will tell you that too, free.

What does “excluding tax” mean for me?

Prices are shown before sales tax or VAT. The tax that applies to you, if any, is added to the quote and shown before you commit, so the number you accept is the number you pay. Business customers in the EU with a VAT number are invoiced under the reverse-charge rule.

How do payments work?

Half to schedule the work, half at launch, by card or bank transfer against an invoice from Veyago Inc. For Back office projects we agree milestones instead. Nothing is charged before you have accepted a written quote.

What if I want changes after launch?

Every package includes revision rounds while we build. After launch, small changes are part of the care plan, and anything larger is quoted as an extra page or a fixed piece of work before we do it. You will never get an hourly bill you did not expect.

Will my site use cookies or trackers?

Not unless you ask for them. We do not add ad trackers, third-party analytics or a cookie banner by default, which is also why the sites are fast. If you need visitor numbers, we can add privacy-respecting analytics that keep your visitors’ data on your own domain.
