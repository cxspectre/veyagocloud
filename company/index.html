About Veyago Inc., an independent New York studio.

Veyago Inc. is an independent New York software studio that builds privacy-first iOS apps and fast, hand-written websites for small businesses. It was incorporated as a New York C-Corporation on 28 April 2026, is run by two people, and ships under two names: the apps and the client website work.

The founder

Why I'm building Veyago.

I kept watching the same thing happen. Friends would spend three weeks arguing in a group chat about where to go, give up, and book the city they went to last year. The travel industry starts the moment you already know your destination - Booking, Skyscanner, Airbnb all begin there. The hard part is before that: the discovery, the disagreement, the distance between how you feel and where you'd actually thrive. Nobody had built for it. So I started Veyago.

The product is mid-rebuild onto a native Flutter client, with health integration underway and internal betas running. I'd rather show you a working bracket than a deck of promises. The goal is a portfolio of software that earns its place on phones because it's genuinely useful at the moments that matter - not because it captures attention. I incorporated Veyago in New York to build it properly, and I'm raising now to ship version one. If that's the kind of thing you back, write about, or build alongside - I'd like to talk.

- Cassian Drefke, Founder & CEO

How we build

A small catalogue. A high bar.

Every product Veyago ships starts with the same question: is there a version of this that's genuinely excellent, private by default, and worth keeping on your phone for years? If the answer is yes, we build it. If it isn't, we don't.

  • Opinionated products

    We build things we couldn't find elsewhere - apps with a point of view that don't compromise it to grow faster. Provisum has no account. Veyago has no ad targeting. Neither will change.

  • Displacement by design

    Discovery and decision first, then planning and operations, then social and memory. We don't compete feature-by-feature - we make the competition redundant a layer at a time.

  • A flywheel rivals can't copy

    Every swipe, bracket, and veto trains a proprietary preference graph no competitor holds. Hopper built a reported $5B valuation on a booking-data flywheel; ours starts a decision earlier. With enough trips, the co-pilot gets too personal to give up.

Provisum · Product overview

Provisum keeps the documents, deadlines, contacts and bills for an ageing parent in one place, and turns them into the packet a hospital, a doctor, an attorney or a bank asks for. It requires no account and stores everything on the device and the owner's own iCloud. One parent's binder is free; a single payment unlocks the rest.

Veyago travel

Veyago travel is being built to help people decide where to go, alone or together. Its Bracket narrows up to 80 destinations to one choice through four rounds. Planned features include group sessions, AI-generated itineraries, and a wellbeing layer. The app is in development for iOS and Android, with launch planned for Q4 2026.
