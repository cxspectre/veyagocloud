Privacy Policy

Veyago travel app & website · Last updated 4 June 2026

In short. Veyago is a travel destination discovery app and website. We collect only what we need to help you discover and plan trips, we never sell your personal data, and you can ask us to access or delete your information at any time. Veyago is a travel destination discovery app and website. This policy explains how Veyago Inc. (“Veyago”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use our mobile apps (iOS and Android) and our website at veyago.app (together, the “Service”).

Data controller

Veyago Inc., a New York corporation with its operational headquarters in Belgium. Contact: hello@veyago.app.

Data protection contact

Until we appoint a formal Data Protection Officer, all privacy questions are handled directly by our CEO and founder, Cassian Drefke, at hello@veyago.app.

UK representative (Article 27 UK GDPR)

If you are in the United Kingdom and wish to contact our UK representative, email hello@veyago.app with “UK Representative” in the subject line and we will route your request accordingly.

The company

Veyago Inc. is a New York business corporation, incorporated April 2026 (DOS ID 7899587). Registered agent for service of process in Albany, NY. Sole founder and 100% shareholder: Cassian Drefke.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

  • Account & contact details — such as your name and email address, if you create an account or contact us.
  • Travel preferences & activity — destinations, searches, saved places, and lists you create, so we can personalize discovery and remember your trips.
  • Approximate or precise location — only when you grant permission, to surface relevant destinations and nearby suggestions. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings.
  • Device & usage data — app version, device type, language, and aggregated, privacy-respecting analytics that help us understand which features are useful and fix problems.
  • Communications — messages you send us and our replies.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Service — discovery, search, saved places, and trip planning.
  • To personalize recommendations and remember your preferences.
  • To respond to your messages and provide support.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and debug issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Contract — to deliver features you ask for.
  • Consent — for optional things like precise location; you can withdraw consent anytime.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve, secure, and maintain the Service in ways you'd reasonably expect.
  • Legal obligation — where the law requires it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf and under contract — for example, cloud hosting, mapping and place data, and analytics — and only as needed to run the Service. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety. If Veyago is ever involved in a merger or acquisition, we will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

International transfers

We operate from the United States and Belgium, so your data may be processed in either region. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize the associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required.

Your rights

Subject to your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email hello@veyago.app. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. If you are a California resident, you may exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights using the same contact; we do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Cookies & analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to keep the site working and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information and to notify you of significant incidents where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. We will post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date, and where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@veyago.app.
