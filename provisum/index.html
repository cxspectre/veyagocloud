A parent’s paperwork, in one place.

Documents, deadlines, contacts and bills for the parent you look after. On your iPhone, shared with your siblings, ready when an office asks.

On the App Store in November

See how it works

One parent. One binder.

Their passport and insurance cards, the power of attorney, the physician’s number, the bills you took over. Everything you keep track of for them lives in one place, and you can find any of it in seconds.

Nothing slips.

The card that expires, the plan that renews, Thursday’s appointment at eleven, the bill due on the fourth. Provisum knows what each one is and tells you in time, on your Lock Screen and in a widget.

Ready before anyone asks.

One tap makes the packet an office actually needs. The emergency packet for a hospital, a packet for a new doctor, one for an attorney, one for a bank. It comes out as a PDF you can hand over, print or send.

Your siblings, on the same page.

Invite a brother or a sister and they see the same binder, take a task and tick it off. Everyone can see what was done and when. They pay nothing.

It stays on your phone.

There is no account to create and no Provisum server holding your parent’s documents. Share the binder with family and it syncs through your own iCloud. The emergency card never leaves your device at all.

Nothing about you or your parent is sent to Veyago. This page loads no trackers either.

Buy it once.

Free for one parent’s binder, including the emergency packet. One payment unlocks everything else, for good. Siblings you invite use it free.

One payment, no subscription. Price at launch.

Questions.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Provisum is an iPhone app. A sibling without an iPhone can still receive any packet you send as a PDF.

What happens to my information if Veyago disappears?

Nothing. Everything is already on your own iPhone and in your own iCloud, and you can export a copy at any time. There is no service to switch off.

Can my parent see their own binder?

Yes, if you invite them the same way you invite a sibling. Provisum does not need your parent to own an iPhone or set anything up.

Does Provisum tell me what to do about my parent’s care?

No. It organises the paperwork and reminds you of dates. Decisions about care, money and legal matters belong with your parent’s doctor, attorney or advisor.

How do I get help?

Write to hello@veyago.cloud or use the support page. A person answers.
