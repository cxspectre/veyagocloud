The founder

Why I'm building Veyago.

I kept watching the same thing happen. Friends would spend three weeks arguing in a group chat about where to go, give up, and book the city they went to last year. The travel industry starts the moment you already know your destination - Booking, Skyscanner, Airbnb all begin there. The hard part is before that: the discovery, the disagreement, the distance between how you feel and where you'd actually thrive. Nobody had built for it. So I started Veyago.

As of June 2026 the product is roughly 42% complete - mid-rebuild onto a native Flutter client, with health integration underway and first internal betas running. I'd rather show you a working bracket than a deck of promises. The goal is a portfolio of software that earns its place on phones because it's genuinely useful at the moments that matter - not because it captures attention. I incorporated Veyago in New York to build it properly, and I'm raising now to ship version one. If that's the kind of thing you back, write about, or build alongside - I'd like to talk.

- Cassian Drefke, Founder & CEO

How we build

A small catalogue. A high bar.

Every product Veyago ships starts with the same question: is there a version of this that's genuinely excellent, private by default, and worth keeping on your phone for years? If the answer is yes, we build it. If it isn't, we don't.

Opinionated products

We build things we couldn't find elsewhere - apps with a point of view that don't compromise it to grow faster. Kept has no account. Veyago has no ad targeting. Neither will change.

Displacement by design

Discovery and decision first, then planning and operations, then social and memory. We don't compete feature-by-feature - we make the competition redundant a layer at a time.

A flywheel rivals can't copy

Every swipe, bracket, and veto trains a proprietary preference graph no competitor holds. Hopper built a reported $5B valuation on a booking-data flywheel; ours starts a decision earlier. With enough trips, the co-pilot gets too personal to give up.

Company description

Veyago Inc. is a New York C-Corporation building a catalogue of private, intelligent apps for iOS and beyond. Its first app, Kept, launches the week of June 8, 2026. Its flagship, Veyago travel, launches Q3 2026. Press contact: hello@veyago.cloud.
Veyago travel

Veyago solves the part of the journey every other app ignores: deciding where to go. At its core is the Bracket - a four-round elimination tournament that narrows up to 80 destinations to one winner, alone or with a live group. Veyago Intelligence then generates the itinerary, finds flights, tracks fares, and reads how the trip will sit with your body. Launching Q3 2026. Press: hello@veyago.cloud.
