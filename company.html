The company

Veyago Inc.

Veyago is a small, independent software studio. We design and build our own apps — and a few for others — with high standards and no dark patterns. We build the kind of software we want to use ourselves: quick, beautiful, and respectful of your time and your data.

Our story

Who we are and why we exist.

Founded in New York

Incorporated as a New York C-Corporation in April 2026, Veyago operates remotely from Belgium — based across two continents, building for people everywhere. Two apps are in flight today, with more to come.

Built for software we wanted to use

Most software has quietly agreed to a bad deal — it harvests your data and treats your attention as inventory. We started Veyago because we didn't want that software, and we suspected we weren't alone. So we build the opposite.

Independent and deliberate

We build slowly, ship when it's right, and hold every release to the same bar: would we want this on our own phone? We'd rather make one thing that endures than ten that don't.

Two apps. More to come.

Veyago travel — the part of a trip the whole industry forgot: deciding where to go. Kept — a private home for the warranties, documents, and renewals that run everyday life. More will follow when they're genuinely good.
