Veyago Inc.

An independent software studio with high standards and no dark patterns. We build calm, private tools that respect your attention and your data.

Get in touch See our apps

Veyago Inc. was founded in New York and operates from Belgium — a small team that cares about the details. We started with a simple belief: software should work for the person using it, not against them. No tricks, no noise, no data harvesting hiding behind a friendly screen.

Today we are building two products. Kept is a calm keeper for anything with a date, launching now on iOS. Veyago is a travel discovery app arriving later this year. Both are designed to feel quiet, considered, and entirely yours.
