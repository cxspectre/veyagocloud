THE COMPANY

Veyago Inc.

Veyago is a small, independent software studio. We design and build our own apps — and a few for others — with high standards and no dark patterns. We build the kind of software we want to use ourselves: quick, beautiful, and respectful of your time and your data.

Where we are

Founded in New York in 2026 and incorporated as a New York C-Corporation, Veyago operates remotely from Belgium — based across two continents, building for people everywhere. Two apps are in flight today, with more to come.

Why we exist

Most software has quietly agreed to a bad deal: it harvests your data, rents you your own files by subscription, and treats your attention as inventory to be sold. We started Veyago because we didn't want to use that software — and we suspected we weren't alone. So we build the opposite. Apps that are genuinely useful, genuinely private, calm to open, and pleasant to live with for years.

How we operate

Independent and deliberate. We build slowly, ship when it's right, and hold every release to the same bar: would we want this on our own phone? We'd rather make one thing that endures than ten that don't. Our business model is honest by design — we charge for software directly, never by selling the people who use it.

What we're building

We started with travel — the part of a trip the whole industry forgot: deciding where to go. That's Veyago travel. Along the way we built Kept, a private home for the warranties, documents, and renewals that run everyday life. Different problems, same DNA: calm, fast, private, and made with care. More apps will follow when they're genuinely good — never to pad a portfolio.
