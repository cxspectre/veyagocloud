Straight answers

What businesses ask first.

What is Veyago?

Veyago Inc. is an independent New York software studio, incorporated in April 2026. It makes its own privacy-first iOS apps and, for businesses anywhere in the world, builds fast websites and back-office tools and runs independent Microsoft 365 and software audits.

How much does a website from Veyago cost?

A one-page Launch site is $699 or €750 excluding tax and is typically live in 7 to 10 working days. A multi-page Business site is $1,690 or €1,790, and a site with a dashboard or CRM behind it starts at $3,900 or €4,100. Every price is fixed in writing before work starts.

Does Veyago do IT audits?

Yes. Fixed-price Microsoft 365 tenant audits from $2,490 and software spend reviews from $1,290, with read-only access and a written report ranked by risk and savings.

What does a Veyago Cockpit cost?

A Cockpit is built as a project, from $3,900 or €4,100 excluding tax, typically in four to eight weeks. On final payment the code and the database are yours, with no license fee afterwards. If you build one within three months of an audit, the audit price comes off.

How quickly do you reply?

Within one working day, from a person. Outages on a site we look after are acknowledged the same working day. Write to hello@veyago.cloud or call +1 (518) 913 2531 in the US, +1 (943) 273 6579 elsewhere.
