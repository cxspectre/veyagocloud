Client access

Your dashboard is coming.

The Veyago dashboard is not open yet. When it is, clients will sign in here to follow a project, read the written scope and price, download invoices, and collect the files and keys we hand over at launch. The studio uses the same place to run the work.

Email us Get support

What it will hold

Everything about your project, in one place.

  • Where the project stands

    The stage it is at, what we are waiting on, and the date we are working to - the same view we use ourselves.

  • Scope and price in writing

    The quote you accepted, what it includes and what it does not, kept where you can read it back at any time.

  • Invoices and payments

    What has been invoiced, what has been paid, and what is outstanding. No chasing an email thread for a number.

  • Your files and keys

    The repository, the hosting account and the domain - the handover, collected in one place rather than sent piecemeal.
