Our approach

Calm software. Honest about your data.

We have a simple belief: good software gets out of your way, respects your attention, and asks as little of your data as possible. Here's how we actually build — including the parts most companies don't put in writing.

Your data is yours

This is the one we won't compromise. We collect the least we need to make a product work, we never sell it, and you can export or delete it whenever you want. That's true whether an app stores nothing at all or needs an account to function.

Privacy, across a spectrum

Different apps need different amounts of data, and we're honest about where each one sits:

  • Kept is at the strict end — no account, no servers of ours, nothing collected. Everything lives on your device and syncs only through your own iCloud. Its privacy label reads "Data Not Collected."
  • Veyago travel needs an account to do its job — saving trips, planning with friends, syncing across devices. So we store the minimum (email, display name, travel preferences), keep precise location off, anonymise our analytics, and never track you across other apps.

Same principle, two honest implementations.

Intelligence that respects you

Most apps send your data to a server to do AI. We avoid that wherever we can — in Kept the smart features run entirely on your device. When intelligence genuinely needs the cloud, like a Veyago itinerary, we route it through our own backend, send only what the task requires, and never hand your identity to advertisers.

No dark patterns

No manufactured urgency, no guilt-trip cancel flows, no burying the off switch, no holding your data hostage behind a renewal. If a design trick only works because it manipulates someone, we don't ship it.

Fair monetisation

We charge for software honestly. Kept is a one-time purchase. Veyago travel is free with a clear, optional subscription. Either way, you are never the thing being sold.

Our principles, in short

  • Your data is yours — collect the least, sell none, export or delete anytime.
  • Privacy-first by default — on-device where we can, minimal-data where we can't.
  • No ad trackers, no cross-app tracking, no precise location unless you ask for it.
  • No dark patterns — ever.
  • Fair, transparent pricing — never by selling you.
  • Fewer features, done properly.
