OUR APPROACH

Calm software. Honest about your data.

We have a simple belief: good software gets out of your way, respects your attention, and asks as little of your data as possible. Here's how we actually build — including the parts most companies don't put in writing.

Your data is yours

This is the one we won't compromise. We collect the least we need to make a product work, we never sell it, and you can export or delete it whenever you want. That's true whether an app stores nothing at all or needs an account to function.

Privacy, across a spectrum

Different apps need different amounts of data, and we're honest about where each one sits:

  • Kept is at the strict end — no account, no servers of ours, nothing collected. Everything lives on your device and syncs only through your own iCloud. Its privacy label reads "Data Not Collected," and we mean it literally.
  • Veyago travel needs an account to do its job — saving your trips, planning with friends in real time, syncing across devices. So we hold ourselves to a different discipline: we store the minimum (your email, a display name, your travel preferences), we keep precise location off — your home city is something you type, not GPS we track — our analytics are anonymised with no personal data attached, and we never track you across other apps or use advertising identifiers.

Same principle, two honest implementations.

Intelligence that respects you

Modern apps lean on AI; most send your data to a server to do it. We avoid that wherever we can. In Kept, the smart features run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device models — nothing leaves your phone. When intelligence genuinely needs the cloud — like generating a travel itinerary in Veyago — we route it through our own backend so your data and the keys stay controlled, send only what the task requires, cache results to reduce exposure, and never hand your identity to advertisers. On-device AI availability varies by device and region.

Honest about third parties

No serious app is an island. To make Veyago work we rely on a few trusted providers — infrastructure to run the service, an AI provider to draft itineraries, anonymised analytics to fix what's broken, and travel partners for booking links. We share only what each one needs, we tell you who they are and why, and none of them get to buy your identity. Kept needs none of this, because it talks to no servers of ours at all.

No dark patterns

No manufactured urgency, no guilt-trip cancel flows, no burying the off switch, no holding your own data hostage behind a renewal. If a design trick only works because it manipulates someone, we don't ship it.

Fair monetisation

We charge for software honestly. Kept is a one-time purchase — pay once, own it. Veyago travel is free to use with a clear, optional subscription and travel booking links. Either way, the deal is on the table and the product is the product — you are never the thing being sold.

Craft and restraint

Good design is mostly deciding what to leave out. Every screen we ship has a job; if it doesn't, it doesn't ship. We sweat the spacing, the wording, and the exact moment a reminder arrives — because calm software is a feature, not a coat of paint.

Built to last

We design apps to still feel right in five years: native on Apple platforms, fast on older devices, accessible by default, and free of the trends that age badly.

Our principles, in short

  • Your data is yours — collect the least, sell none, export or delete anytime.
  • Privacy-first by default — on-device where we can, minimal-data where we can't.
  • No ad trackers, no cross-app tracking, no precise location unless you ask for it.
  • Honest about third parties — minimum shared, clearly disclosed.
  • No dark patterns — ever.
  • Fair, transparent pricing — never by selling you.
  • Fewer features, done properly.
  • Software that respects your time and your attention.
