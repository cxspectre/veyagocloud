The Studio

We build software we'd actually want to use.

Most apps treat your attention as something to capture and your data as something to harvest. We build the opposite — apps that are quick, beautiful, genuinely useful, and respectful of your time and your data. Small catalogue, high standards, no dark patterns.

iOS & multiplatform engineering

Applied intelligence

Privacy architecture

Local-first where we can, minimal-data where we can't. Designed so there's little to leak and nothing to sell.

Our services

How we build

Your data is yours.

We collect the least we need, never sell it, and let you take it or delete it whenever you want. Some of our apps keep everything on your device with no account at all. Others need an account to work — so we minimise what we store, anonymise our analytics, and never track you across apps.
