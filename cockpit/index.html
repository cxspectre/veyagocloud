Veyago Cockpit · For growing businesses

One place to run your business.

Veyago Cockpit is the private workspace Veyago runs its own business on. Mail, calendar, clients, deals, projects, support and invoices, in one place and on one database. We build one around the way your business works.

Talk about your Cockpit See what's inside

What's inside

Seven tools. One sign-in.

Everything a client touches, from the first email to the paid invoice.

  • Mail & agenda

    Your Microsoft 365 inbox and calendar, synced both ways. Reply, file and invite from next to the client record.

  • Clients & deals

    Companies, contacts and several deals per client, each with its own stage and value. A website enquiry becomes a lead in one click.

  • Projects

    Tasks, files, budget and the client's own people. Progress is counted from the tasks, so it never drifts.

  • Support tickets

    Numbered tickets from the first message to resolved. Replies go out by email, and you can see they arrived.

  • Finance

    Invoices with their PDFs, bank and Stripe transactions, revenue by month. Visible only to the people you choose.

  • Overview

    The four numbers that matter today, the week ahead and what the team just did.

Deals

Every deal, on one board.

From lead to won, with the value and the next step on every card. Move a deal forward and the whole team sees it move.

Agenda

The week, with your calendar in it.

Meetings from Microsoft 365 sit next to the work that is due. Create an event here and the invitation goes out from your own address.

Finance

The money, for the people who need it.

Open invoices, what is overdue and revenue by month, in your own currency. Shown to the roles you pick, hidden from everyone else.

Straight answers

Before you ask.

Is Cockpit a subscription?

No. We build your Cockpit as a project, on the same foundations we run Veyago on, and on final payment the code and the database are yours. It runs in hosting accounts in your name, so there is no licence fee to Veyago.

Who can see our data?

Only the people you let in. Cockpit runs on a database in your own account, every table is private by default, and each person's role decides what they can read. Finance stays with the people you name.

Does it work with the tools we already use?

Mail and calendar come from Microsoft 365, payments from Stripe and bank transactions from Mercury - those connections run in our own Cockpit today. Anything else is looked at during scoping, and the quote says in writing what will connect.

How many people can use it?

Everyone on your team. Each person signs in with a second factor, and their role decides what they see - a new hire gets their own tickets and projects on day one, not the company accounts.

Can we start with only part of it?

Yes, and most businesses should. Start with clients and deals, or with projects and invoicing, and add the rest when you need it. Everything sits on one database, so a later module never means a migration.

What does it cost?

Cockpit is built as part of our Back office package, from $3,900 or €4,100 excluding tax, typically in four to eight weeks. The scope and the price are fixed in writing before any work starts.
