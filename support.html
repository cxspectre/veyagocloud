Common questions

Quick answers.

How do I delete my data?

In Kept, delete any item in the app, or delete the app to remove its local data; iCloud data is managed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. For Veyago travel, you'll be able to export or delete your account from within the app. See our privacy policies.

Is Kept free?

Kept is a one-time purchase - pay once, own it. No subscription.

When does Veyago travel launch?

Q3 2026. Get notified and we'll email you the day it's live.
