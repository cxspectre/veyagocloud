The team

The two people who build Veyago.

Veyago Inc. is two people: Cassian Drefke, founder and CEO, who designs and writes the software, and Wessel Gelderblom, vice president, who runs strategy and delivery. There is nobody else between you and the work.

Who we are

The people building it.

Founder & CEO

Cassian Drefke

Cassian founded Veyago in New York in April 2026 with a single brief: build the apps he couldn't find anywhere else. He leads product strategy, design and company direction, and wrote the brief that became both Kept and the Veyago travel app.

He also does the building. Kept ships as native Swift on Apple platforms; the studio's client websites are hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Supabase behind the ones that need a login. He writes the studio's working papers, The Unkept Life and The Edge Moves In, and the field notes, and he is the person who answers hello@veyago.cloud. Based in New York.

Works on: iOS and Swift, on-device machine learning, privacy architecture, static site engineering, technical SEO.

Vice President

Wessel Gelderblom

Wessel leads strategy and operations. He scopes the work before a quote goes out, keeps a project on the dates it was promised, and runs the handover at the end so a client leaves with the repository, the hosting account and the documentation in their own name.

On client projects he is the second pair of eyes on scope: what belongs in a package, what is genuinely an extra, and when the honest answer is that a rebuild is not worth it. He works with Cassian across both the app catalogue and the studio's website work.

Works on: product operations, project delivery, client strategy, scoping and handover.

How we work

A studio, not a factory.

Small by design. We don't scale headcount ahead of quality - we'd rather build two products we're proud of than ten we're not. Outside of the core team, we work with a small number of trusted collaborators on specific projects and capabilities.

  • Founder-led

    Every product decision flows from the people who'll live with it. No delegation of vision, no product managers between the idea and the build.

  • Selective on outside work

    We occasionally take on a small number of product and engineering projects for outside teams. Same standards, same team. See services

Building together

Interested in what we're building?

We're always open to conversations with people who share our standards - whether you want to back us, write about us, build with us, or build something together.
