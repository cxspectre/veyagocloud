Questions, bug reports, feedback, or account help - we read every message and usually reply within a day or two. The fastest way to reach us is email.

How to reach us

Email hello@veyago.cloud with “Website support” in the subject line - best for anything with a screenshot. If the site is down, call: +1 (518) 913 2531 from the US, +1 (943) 273 6579 from anywhere else. We keep New York hours, Monday to Friday.

How fast we reply

An outage - the site is down, or a form no longer reaches you - is acknowledged the same working day. Every other request gets a reply within one working day. Working days are Monday to Friday, New York time.

What the care plan covers

Updates, uptime monitoring, backups, and small changes - a new photo, a changed opening time, a line of text. It is monthly and you can cancel at any time. Larger changes are quoted before we do them. Price and details are on the Websites page.

Something is wrong with your site

Send the site’s address, what you see, and a screenshot if you can, to hello@veyago.cloud. Not on the care plan? Write anyway - we will tell you what the fix takes before we do anything, and quote it if it is not small.

Common questions

Quick answers.

How do I delete my data?

In Kept, delete any item in the app, or delete the app to remove its local data; iCloud data is managed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. For Veyago travel, you'll be able to export or delete your account from within the app. See our privacy policies.

Is Kept free?

Yes. Kept is free to download and free to use, with no account and no advertising. A one-time $29.99 Lifetime unlock adds the full feature set if you want it. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

How much does Kept cost?

Kept costs nothing to download. The only paid option is Kept Lifetime at $29.99, charged once through the App Store. Veyago travel will be free to download when it launches in Q4 2026.

Does Veyago work on Android?

Kept is iPhone only and is on the Apple App Store. Veyago travel is planned for iPhone and Android at launch in Q4 2026. There is no Android build of Kept.

When does Veyago travel launch?

Q4 2026. Get notified and we'll email you the day it's live.

I think I found a bug.

Please tell us - email hello@veyago.cloud with what happened, your device, and your iOS version. It genuinely helps.

Where are your privacy policies?

The Veyago travel privacy policy and the Kept privacy policy.

You built my website and something is wrong. Who do I contact?

Email hello@veyago.cloud with the site’s address and what you see, or call +1 (518) 913 2531 (US) or +1 (943) 273 6579 (international) if the site is down. Outages are acknowledged the same working day, everything else within one working day. See Website clients above.
