How to reach us

Email hello@veyago.cloud with “Website support” in the subject line - best for anything with a screenshot. If the site is down, call: +1 (518) 913 2531 from the US, +1 (943) 273 6579 from anywhere else. We keep New York hours, Monday to Friday.

How fast we reply

An outage - the site is down, or a form no longer reaches you - is acknowledged the same working day. Every other request gets a reply within one working day. Working days are Monday to Friday, New York time.

What the care plan covers

Updates, uptime monitoring, backups, and small changes - a new photo, a changed opening time, a line of text. It is monthly and you can cancel at any time. Larger changes are quoted before we do them. Price and details are on the Websites page.

Something is wrong with your site

Send the site’s address, what you see, and a screenshot if you can, to hello@veyago.cloud. Not on the care plan? Write anyway - we will tell you what the fix takes before we do anything, and quote it if it is not small.

Provisum

Help with Provisum.

Write to hello@veyago.cloud. Tell us which device you are on, which version of iOS it is running, and what happened - those three things usually turn a second email into no second email.

A person reads every message and replies within one working day, Monday to Friday, New York time. If something is broken for everyone, we say so the same working day rather than answering each message separately.

I invited my sister and she cannot see the binder.

The invitation travels through Apple's iCloud sharing, so she has to open it on a device signed in to her own Apple Account, and she needs iOS 26 or later. If the invitation has gone stale, remove her from the people tab and invite her again. Nothing about the binder passes through us, so there is nothing for us to re-send from our side.

A document would not scan properly.

Photograph it flat, in even light, with the whole page inside the frame - the edges are what the app uses to straighten the page. If the text still comes out wrong you can type the details in by hand and keep the photograph as it is; nothing is lost either way. Send us the kind of document it was and we will look at it.

Where does an exported copy go?

Wherever you send it. An export is a PDF and the files you put in, handed to the iOS share sheet: save it to Files, put it in your own iCloud Drive, email it, or print it. It does not pass through Veyago and we never see it.

How do I get a refund?

Through Apple, not through us. Every purchase is made through the App Store, which means Apple takes the payment and Apple issues any refund - ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase we never received, and we would rather say so plainly than have you wait on us. Tell us why, though: if something is wrong with the app, we want to fix it.

Common questions

Quick answers.

How do I delete my data?

In Provisum, delete a binder to remove it from your device, your iCloud and anyone you shared it with, or delete the app to remove its local data; iCloud data is managed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. For Veyago travel, you'll be able to export or delete your account from within the app. See our privacy policies.

Is Provisum free?

One parent's binder is free, including the emergency packet, with no account and no advertising. A single payment unlocks everything else and never renews. The price is announced when the app arrives in November 2026.

How much does Provisum cost?

Provisum costs nothing to download and the free binder stays free. The only paid option is a single unlock, charged once through the App Store, with the price announced at launch. Veyago travel will be free to download when it launches in Q4 2026.

Does Veyago work on Android?

Provisum is for iPhone and iPad, on iOS 26 and later. Veyago travel is planned for iPhone and Android at launch in Q4 2026. There is no Android build of Provisum.

When does Veyago travel launch?

Q4 2026. Get notified and we'll email you the day it's live.

I think I found a bug.

Please tell us - email hello@veyago.cloud with what happened, your device, and your iOS version. It genuinely helps.

Where are your privacy policies?

The Veyago travel privacy policy and the Provisum privacy notice.
