Kept Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 June 2026

The short version. Kept keeps your data on your own devices and your private iCloud. We don't collect it, we can't see it, we don't track you, and we never sell anything. There are no accounts and no Veyago servers.

Kept is built and published by Veyago Inc. (“Veyago”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what Kept does — and doesn't do — with your information.

The data Kept handles

Everything you add to Kept — items, receipts, documents, photos, prices, notes, and reminders — is stored locally on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, this data syncs across your own Apple devices through Apple's CloudKit using your private iCloud database.

  • We do not operate servers that receive your content.
  • We have no access to your items, photos, or documents.
  • Your data is not shared with us or any third party.

Camera & photos

When you scan a receipt or import a photo, Kept reads the image on your device to extract details such as the store, total, and date. Images and the text recognized from them stay on your device (and your private iCloud, if enabled). They are never uploaded to us.

On-device intelligence (“Ask Kept”)

On supported devices, Kept's assistant uses Apple's on-device foundation models (Apple Intelligence). Your questions and item data are processed on your device. Kept does not send your data to Veyago or to any external AI service.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server. (Apple's iCloud sync uses Apple's own mechanisms, which never expose your content to us.)

Purchases

Kept offers a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details. Purchase validation happens on your device through Apple's StoreKit.

Analytics & tracking

Kept contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We do not use the Advertising Identifier, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Data retention & deletion

Because your data lives on your devices and your iCloud, you are in control:

  • Delete any item in the app to remove it.
  • Delete the app to remove its local data from a device.
  • Manage or delete iCloud data via Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

Kept is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. (Kept does not collect personal data from anyone.)

Your rights

Because Veyago does not collect or process your personal data through Kept, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete — your content is controlled entirely by you through your device and Apple iCloud. For data Apple processes (iCloud and the App Store), please see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Kept evolves. We'll post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date.
