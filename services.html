WHAT WE DO

Design, engineering, and everything between.

One studio across the full arc of a product — strategy, craft, and launch.

✦

Product design

Interfaces with taste and restraint. We design the flows, the screens, and the details — and we know what to cut. Strategy, UX, UI, design systems, and ready-to-build prototypes.

⌘

iOS & multiplatform engineering

Native, fast, and reliable. We build in Swift across Apple platforms — iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch — with architecture meant to last, not just to ship. Widgets, App Intents, and deep system integration included. For cross-platform work, we build with modern frameworks that keep one codebase honest across iOS and Android.

✨

Applied intelligence

Useful AI without the surveillance. We build smart features that run on the user's device wherever possible, and route cloud AI responsibly through a controlled backend when it's genuinely needed — minimal data, no advertising identities, no leaks.

🔒

Privacy & data architecture

Local-first where we can, minimal-data where we can't. We help teams design products that are private by default — GDPR-ready, with data export and deletion, anonymised analytics, encryption in transit, and strict access controls — so they can honestly say their users' data is safe.

◆

App Store presence & launch

Getting built is half the job. We handle store listing, ASO, screenshots, and the kind of organic launch plan that gets an app found without burning a marketing budget.

◷

How we work

Small, senior, hands-on. One studio from sketch to ship — no handoffs, no layers. We take on projects that share our standards: calm software, real craft, and respect for the people who use it.
