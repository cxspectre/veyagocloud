What we do

Everything a product needs.

A focused set of disciplines that work better together — so nothing falls through the gaps between design, code, and launch.

✦

Product design

Interfaces with taste and restraint. We map the flow, draw every screen, and cut anything that doesn't earn its place.

⌘

iOS & multiplatform engineering

Native Swift on Apple platforms — fast, reliable, and built to age well. SwiftUI, SwiftData, and the frameworks that matter.

✨

On-device intelligence

Smart features powered by Apple Intelligence. Scanning, extraction, and natural-language help that run on the device, not in a cloud.

🔒

Privacy architecture

Local-first storage and private iCloud sync, designed so there's simply nothing to leak. No accounts, no servers, no trackers.

◆

Brand & identity

A coherent voice and look — name, icon, type, and tone — so the product feels like one considered thing from the App Store to the first tap.

✚

App Store launch

Listing, screenshots, metadata, and review readiness. We handle the details that get an app approved and ready for its first users.

How we work

From idea to App Store.

A calm, four-step rhythm. Small, opinionated, and close to the work — no hand-offs to teams who never read the brief.

1

Discover

We get clear on who it's for and what it must do. The fewest features that make the product genuinely useful, and an honest scope to match.

2

Design

Flows, screens, and a brand that fit together. We prototype the real thing early so decisions are made by looking, not arguing.

3

Build

Native engineering with on-device intelligence and a privacy-first foundation. Shippable from day one, refined every week.

4

Ship

App Store submission, launch assets, and the polish that comes last. Then we stay close to keep it fast and reliable.
