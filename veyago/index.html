The travel app · Coming Q4 2026

Veyago travel: decide where to go, together.

Veyago travel is a trip-decision app for iPhone and Android, arriving in Q4 2026. It turns "where should we go?" into an answer: swipe through up to 80 destinations, bracket them down to one winner alone or live with your group, then let Veyago Intelligence build the itinerary.

Free to download when it lands, with an optional Premium subscription. Not to be confused with Veyago Inc., the New York studio that makes it, or with Kept, its iPhone app for warranties and receipts.

Join the waitlist Visit veyago.app

How it works

From a hundred maybes to one trip.

1

Swipe to discover

Up to 80 destinations, ranked to your taste by Veyago Intelligence - each with a short reason it surfaced for you.

2

Bracket to decide

Four fast rounds narrow 80 → 20 → 10 → 5 → one. Vote on your own, or open a live session and let your group eliminate down to a single winner together.

3

Intelligence does the rest

The moment a winner lands, Veyago builds the day-by-day itinerary, finds the flights, watches the fare, and packs the list. Decision to departure, handled.

The Bracket & group sessions

Stop scrolling. Start eliminating.

A four-round elimination tournament that mirrors how people decide - by comparing, not agonising alone. Up to eighty candidates collapse to one winner, quick and a little addictive. Run it solo, or open a live group session with a "strong consensus" signal when you're aligned. The last travel app you'll ever argue over.

Veyago Intelligence™

An ambient intelligence, not a chatbot.

A quiet layer running under every screen - surfacing a reason here, a summary there, the right nudge at the right moment. You never have to open a chat window and ask.

Reads the moment

Why a place is in your deck, what a bracket round revealed, a two-line destination brief, your itinerary's next move - small, evidence-backed touches woven through the app, never in your way.

Runs anywhere

A layered engine routes between the cloud, Apple Intelligence on your device, and a local model - with a deterministic fallback, so it's never blank, even fully offline.

Grounded, not guessed

Every suggestion is checked against real place data before it reaches you. Verified facts get a badge - so what you read about a place is real, not invented.

The Wellbeing Layer

Plan trips your body can actually take.

Travel is physical - jet lag, thin sleep, altitude, twelve-hour days. Veyago reads recovery, sleep, and activity from Apple Health or Health Connect: a jet-lag forecast for your exact route, lighter days when your recovery is low, acclimatisation guidance above 2,500 m. Health data never leaves your device.

The Explorer Map

A world map that fills itself in.

Every trip - and every place you've already photographed - quietly lights up your own world map. Countries explored, routes travelled, continents unlocked. Milestones, streaks, and a growing footprint of everywhere you've been. The quiet reward for all that wandering.

Questions

What people ask before they join.

When does Veyago travel launch?

Q4 2026. Join the waitlist and we will email you the day it is live - once, to say so, and nothing else.

How much will Veyago travel cost?

It will be free to download, with a genuinely useful free tier rather than a crippled demo. An optional Premium subscription is €4.99 a month or €29.99 a year.

Will Veyago travel be on Android?

Yes. Veyago travel is planned for both iPhone and Android at launch. Kept, our other app, is iPhone only.

How does the Bracket work?

Four fast rounds narrow up to 80 destinations down to one: 80 to 20, then 10, then 5, then a single winner. You can vote alone, or open a live session and have your group eliminate down to one together.

Does Veyago travel need an account?

Yes, unlike Kept. It stores your email, display name and travel preferences on our server. Health and activity data, calendar access and location are processed on your device and never stored server-side, and one click deletes everything we hold.

Is Veyago travel the same thing as Veyago Inc.?

No. Veyago Inc. is the New York studio that makes it, and owns this site. Veyago travel is one of its two apps and has its own site at veyago.app.
