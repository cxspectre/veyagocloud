VEYAGO · PRESS NOTES
Updated 18 September 2026 | Based on the studio website

COMPANY
Veyago Inc. is an independent New York software studio founded by Cassian Drefke in April 2026. The two-person studio builds privacy-first apps and fast, hand-written websites for small businesses. Kept is available on the App Store; Veyago travel is in development for a planned Q4 2026 launch.

KEPT
Kept helps people organise warranties, documents, subscriptions, and expiry dates, with reminders before anything lapses. Available on the App Store, it requires no account and stores data on the device. It is free to download, with a $29.99 one-time unlock.

VEYAGO TRAVEL
Veyago travel is being built to help people decide where to go, alone or together. Its Bracket narrows up to 80 destinations to one choice through four rounds. Planned features include group sessions, AI-generated itineraries, and a wellbeing layer. The app is in development for iOS and Android, with launch planned for Q4 2026.
